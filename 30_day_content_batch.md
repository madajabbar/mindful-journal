Journaling vs. Talking: When to Use Which? 🤔

✍️ **Journaling** is better when:
• You need clarity
• Emotions are tangled and need untangling
• It's 2 AM and you don't want to bother friends
• You want to track emotional patterns

🗣️ **Talking** is better when:
• You need another perspective
• There's a problem needing concrete solutions
• You feel isolated and need connection
• The situation requires professional support

Both matter. You don't have to choose one. 💚

🧠 **Naming Emotions = Reducing Intensity**
