Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Mindful Journal is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what you voluntarily provide: journal entries, mood data, habit records, and app settings. We do NOT collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any personally identifiable information by default.

3. Data Storage

Local First: All data is stored locally on your device using encrypted Hive database. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync.

Cloud (Optional): If enabled, data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest in Firebase Firestore. Only you can access your synced data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (optional): Cloud sync & analytics — Privacy
  • Google Play Services: App distribution — Privacy
  • Google AdMob: Non-intrusive banner ads — Privacy

5. Your Rights

You can access, modify, export, and delete all your data at any time through Settings → Data Management. We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
