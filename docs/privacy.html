Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Mindful Journal ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using the App:

  • Journal entries: Personal reflections, thoughts, and daily notes
  • Mood data: Emotional state records and related notes
  • Habit tracking data: Daily habits and completion records
  • Settings and preferences: App configuration choices

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Device information: Device type, operating system version
  • Usage data: Feature usage patterns, session duration (anonymous)
  • App performance data: Crash reports and error logs (anonymous)

2.3 Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect personally identifiable information (PII) by default
  • We do NOT collect your name, email, or phone number
  • We do NOT collect your location data
  • We do NOT access your contacts, photos, or other personal files
  • We do NOT collect microphone or camera data

3. How We Store Your Data

3.1 Local Storage (Primary)

  • All journal entries, mood data, and habit records are stored locally on your device
  • We use Hive, a secure local database, to store your information
  • Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud sync
  • Local data is encrypted using Hive's built-in encryption

3.2 Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you choose to enable Firebase cloud sync (optional feature):

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Cloud data is stored in Firebase Firestore with Firebase Security Rules
  • Only you can access your cloud-synced data through authenticated access
  • You can delete cloud data at any time through app settings

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide core functionality: Journaling, mood tracking, and habit building
  • Generate insights: Personalized wellness analytics and patterns
  • Improve the App: Identify usage patterns to enhance features
  • Send reminders: Daily notifications for journaling and habits (if enabled)
  • Debug and fix issues: Anonymous crash reports to improve stability

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We DO NOT share, sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.

We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you explicitly choose to share data (e.g., export)
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • Service providers: Anonymous analytics data to improve app performance

6. Third-Party Services

Our App may use the following third-party services:

6.1 Firebase (Optional)

  • Purpose: Cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Data Shared: Only if you enable cloud sync feature

6.2 Google Play Services

  • Purpose: App distribution, analytics, crash reporting
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6.3 Google AdMob

  • Purpose: Banner advertising to support free features
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Data Shared: Anonymous device identifiers for ad targeting

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data:

  • Local encryption: Hive database with AES-256 encryption
  • Secure deletion: Data is permanently removed when you delete it
  • No network transmission: Your data stays on your device by default
  • Optional cloud encryption: End-to-end encryption for cloud sync

8. Data Retention and Deletion

8.1 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Export all your data as PDF/CSV files
  • Delete your data: Remove all data from the app at any time
  • Modify your data: Edit or update any information
  • Data portability: Take your data and use it elsewhere

8.2 How to Delete Your Data

  1. Open the app
  2. Go to Settings → Data Management
  3. Tap "Clear Local Data"
  4. Confirm deletion

This will permanently delete all your journal entries, mood data, and habits from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Our App is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it.

10. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We may collect anonymous, aggregated data to understand app usage patterns, improve performance, and fix bugs. This data does NOT include your journal entries, mood data, or personal information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
